# mehmetcinar.com

Mehmet Cinar's personal Site, where he publishes in English what he builds and what he learns.

## Language

**Site**:
mehmetcinar.com as a whole: every page, feed and generated image.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, blog, website

**Post**:
A dated piece of writing published in the feed, with a title, a description and Tags.
_Avoid_: Article, blog post, entry

**Draft**:
A Post with `draft: true`. It lives in the repo but is not published.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, WIP

**Project**:
Something Mehmet built, presented on the Projects page. It has no publication date.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, work, case study

**Tag**:
A label that groups Posts by subject. Today: `electricity` and `dev`.
_Avoid_: Category, topic

**Series**:
An ordered sequence of Posts that follow one another, such as the stages of one piece of
electrical work.
_Avoid_: Collection, saga, part

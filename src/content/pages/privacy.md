This Site is run by me, Mehmet Cinar, a private person in Belgium. For any question about your
data, write to [hello@mehmetcinar.com](mailto:hello@mehmetcinar.com).

## Visiting the Site

The Site is hosted on Cloudflare. I count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no
cookie and does not track you from one site to another. I see totals only: pages viewed,
countries, referring sites, browsers.

One cookie, `theme`, remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. It is set only when you
use the theme switcher, and it is shared with my apps on `*.mehmetcinar.com` so they open in the
same theme.

## The newsletter

If you subscribe, I keep your email address to send you one email when a new Post is out. The
legal basis is your consent: you give it by subscribing and confirming from the email you
receive.

- **Who handles it.** The list is stored and the emails are sent by
  [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com), in the United States, under a data processing agreement
  with the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
- **What is collected.** Your email address, the date you subscribed and confirmed, and whether
  an email to you bounced. Opens and clicks are not tracked.
- **How long.** Until you unsubscribe. Addresses that stop working are removed automatically.
- **Unsubscribing.** Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also write to me and I
  will remove you.

## Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct or delete your data, or to stop using it. If you think I handle
your data wrongly, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority
([dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)).
